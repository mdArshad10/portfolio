# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, freelance clients, and anyone discovering the developer's digital presence. Their context is evaluation—they are looking to quickly understand the developer's capabilities, past work, and technical depth.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio website acting as a digital resume and project showcase. It exists to demonstrate the developer's skills and make it as easy as possible for clients to hand off projects or recruiters to offer roles. Success means high conversion from visitor to contact/lead.

## Brand Personality
Technical, precise, and professional. It should feel uniquely owned by the developer, projecting quiet confidence and engineering rigor.

## Anti-references
- No cartoonish animations
- No overly complex 3D graphics
- No generic templates
- No funky color gradients

## Design Principles
- **Technical Precision:** The interface should feel deliberate, fast, and exact, reflecting the developer's engineering mindset.
- **Content as the Hero:** Projects and skills must take center stage. The design frames the work without competing with it.
- **Professional Clarity:** Navigation and information architecture must be immediately obvious to busy recruiters and clients.
- **Unique but Grounded:** Establish a distinct identity through sharp typography, crisp layouts, and spacing, avoiding tired templates while remaining highly functional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard web accessibility best practices (WCAG). Ensure high contrast, readable typography, and semantic structure for screen readers, ensuring all users can evaluate the work without friction.
